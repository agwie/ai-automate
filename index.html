// THE_PROBLEM

Client chats don’t scale with headcount

Support and sales teams spend their day in messengers answering the same questions, looking for messages they missed and checking documents by hand. Hiring more people only makes the inbox bigger.

01 / REPEATS

The same questions every day

Payments, documents, deadlines — typed out again and again by different people.

02 / MISSED

Messages get lost

Dozens of chats, and nobody can see which ones still need a reply.

03 / RISK

One wrong answer is expensive

Legal and financial questions get answered from memory instead of from approved wording.

04 / KNOWLEDGE

Answers depend on who replies

New managers ask colleagues, and the same question gets different answers.

// HOW_IT_WORKS

From incoming message
to approved reply

The copilot works inside the chats your team already uses. Clients talk to people, the copilot prepares everything around the reply.

Step 01

Reads

Every incoming message with the conversation around it: text, documents, photos. Voice notes are transcribed first.

Step 02

Understands

Works out the topic, whether a reply is needed and what exactly the client is asking for.

Step 03

Checks

Red-zone requests — legal, financial, disputes — go straight to a manager with the reason attached.

Step 04

Drafts

A reply built from your approved answers and live client data, delivered as a card to the managers’ group.

Step 05

Manager approves

Send as is, edit or reject. Every decision is logged and used to measure and improve the copilot.
